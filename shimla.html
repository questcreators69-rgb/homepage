My Favorite Vacation

Shimla is my absolute favorite trip so far! The cool mountain weather, pine trees, and beautiful green hills made it an unforgettable holiday with my family. We enjoyed walking around the Mall Road, taking pictures of the valley, and looking at the mountains in the distance.
